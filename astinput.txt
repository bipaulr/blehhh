# Operators are +, -, *, /; parentheses control grouping; unary minus is unsupported.
# The first tree has + at its root and * as its right child.
a+b*c
# The second tree has * at its root and + as its left child.
(a+b)*5

# After each tree the preorder and postorder are printed.

# (a+b)*5 -> Preorder: * + a b 5    Postorder: a b + 5 *
