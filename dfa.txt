MINIMIZE A DFA: TABLE FILLING

ALGORITHM
1.  Mark a pair of states as different if exactly one of them is final.
2.  Repeat: if, on some symbol, a pair goes to a pair already marked different, mark it too. Stop when nothing
 changes.
3.  Pairs never marked are equivalent. Merge each group under its smallest state number.
4.  Print each group with its moves pointing at group names.
