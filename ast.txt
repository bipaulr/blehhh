BNF TO YACC: ABSTRACT SYNTAX TREE

ALGORITHM
1.  Each BNF rule becomes one yacc rule (expr, term, factor). The three layers make * and / bind tighter than +
 and -.
2.  A tree node stores a label and two child pointers. make_node(label, left, right) creates one.
3.  Operator rule: $$ = make_node(op, $1, $3). Name or number: a leaf node with no children. Brackets: just
 pass $2 up.
4.  print_tree visits right child, then the node, then left child, indenting 4 spaces per level.
5.  preorder visits root, left, right. postorder visits left, right, root. Both are printed after the tree.
